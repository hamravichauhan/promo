DAY 33 / 100: Mobile Forms: The 2-Input Rule That Triples Lead Submissions

Typing on a glass keyboard is high friction. Every extra field halves your conversion.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / Ask Only for Name & Work Email
  You can always collect project scope, budget, and timeline in the automated follow-up email.

• 02 / Enable Mobile Autofill
  Use proper HTML autocomplete tags: autocomplete='name' and autocomplete='email'.

• 03 / Set Correct Keyboard Types
  Use type='tel' for phone numbers to trigger the big numeric keypad automatically.
