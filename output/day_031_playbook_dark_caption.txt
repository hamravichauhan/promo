DAY 31 / 100: The Natural Thumb Zone: Designing for One-Handed Mobile Browsing

Over 75% of your traffic holds their phone in one hand. Place buttons where their thumb lives.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / Sticky Bottom CTA Bar
  Pin your primary 'Contact Us' or 'Book Call' button to the bottom 80px on mobile screens.

• 02 / 48px Minimum Touch Target
  Never make buttons smaller than 48px high to eliminate frustrating mis-taps.

• 03 / Keep Destructive Actions Away
  Place cancel or close buttons in the upper stretch zone so users don't tap by mistake.
