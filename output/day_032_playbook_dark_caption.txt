DAY 32 / 100: Why Shrinking Desktop Layouts Is Not Real Responsive Design

Mobile-first means rethinking information density, not just stacking 4 columns into 1.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / Replace Tables with Card Stacks
  Wide comparison tables are unreadable on 390px screens. Convert each row into a distinct card.

• 02 / Use Horizontal Swipes for Proof
  Allow users to swipe through client logos and reviews rather than stacking them vertically.

• 03 / Truncate Long Explanations
  Use 'Read more' accordions so mobile visitors aren't trapped in endless scrolls.
