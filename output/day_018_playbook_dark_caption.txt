DAY 18 / 100: Hick's Law: Why Offering 3 Competing Buttons Destroys Sales

The time it takes to make a decision increases logarithmically with the number of options.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / One Primary Goal Per Page
  Every page should have one singular desired outcome—demote all other actions.

• 02 / Visual Hierarchy on Secondary Actions
  Use an outline ghost button or plain text link for secondary options.

• 03 / Guide, Don't Confuse
  Lead the visitor by the hand rather than making them deliberate on what to click.
