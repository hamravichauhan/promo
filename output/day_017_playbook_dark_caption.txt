DAY 17 / 100: Above-the-Fold Blueprint: The 4 Must-Have Hero Elements

Over 60% of all user attention occurs before the very first scroll.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / Standout Primary CTA
  Your button must use a contrasting accent color that appears nowhere else above the fold.

• 02 / Quantitative Social Proof
  Include 'Trusted by 500+ Businesses' or '★ 4.9/5 Average Rating' directly below the CTA.

• 03 / Supporting Subheading
  One 2-line sentence detailing how you fulfill the main headline's promise.
