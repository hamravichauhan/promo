DAY 10 / 100: SSR vs CSR: Why Single-Page Apps Suffer in SEO & Speed

Showing a blank white screen while downloading a 2MB bundle destroys conversion.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / Migrate to Next.js or Astro
  Pre-render static content to HTML while hydrating interactivity progressively.

• 02 / Stream Slow Components
  Use React Suspense to render fast page shells while slower database widgets load.

• 03 / Never Block Initial Render
  Initial HTML must contain readable text and critical branding instantly.
