DAY 20 / 100: Social Proof Placement: Stop Hiding Testimonials at the Bottom

Objections arise immediately. Reassurance must be present where objections occur.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / Place Client Logos Directly Below Hero
  Show 4-5 recognizable client logos or trust badges right under the main CTA.

• 02 / Use Specific Numbers in Quotes
  'They increased our leads by 142%' beats 'Great service, highly recommend!' every time.

• 03 / Include Headshot, Name & Title
  Real photos and verified LinkedIn titles eliminate skepticism.
