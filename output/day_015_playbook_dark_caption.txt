DAY 15 / 100: The Google Lighthouse 100/100 Checklist: What Actually Counts

Don't chase vanity numbers—focus on the 3 metrics that drive real business growth.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / LCP is 25% of Your Score
  Your hero image or primary headline must paint in under 1.2 seconds.

• 02 / TBT (Total Blocking Time) is 30%
  Eliminate heavy scripts executing during the first 3 seconds of page load.

• 03 / Accessibility is Free SEO
  Adding alt tags, semantic HTML tags, and ARIA labels lifts both UX and search rank.
