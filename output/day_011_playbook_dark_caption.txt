DAY 11 / 100: Third-Party Script Audit: Who Is Really Slowing Down Your Site?

Heatmaps, chat widgets, and 4 different analytics tags silently kill page speed.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / Use Partytown
  Run third-party scripts (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel) in a web worker off the main thread.

• 02 / Remove Zombie Scripts
  Old marketing campaigns frequently leave unused tracking pixels running forever.

• 03 / Load Chat Widgets on Intent
  Replace heavy Intercom or Crisp embeds with a lightweight button that loads on click.
