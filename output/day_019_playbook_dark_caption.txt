DAY 19 / 100: The F-Pattern Reading Flow: How Visitors Actually Scan

Eye-tracking studies prove users read web pages in an F-shaped trajectory.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / Front-Load Keywords
  Put the most compelling value words at the very beginning of headlines and bullets.

• 02 / Keep Critical Actions Left-Aligned
  Center-aligned long text slows reading speed; left-alignment matches natural eye drift.

• 03 / Use Bold Lead-In Words
  Start every list item with 2-3 bold words to catch scanning eyes.
