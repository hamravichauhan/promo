DAY 16 / 100: The 5-Second Rule: Can Visitors Tell What You Sell Instantly?

Visitors don't read; they glance. If your hero is confusing, they bounce.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / Answer 3 Questions Instantly
  Who is this for? What problem does it solve? What action do I take next?

• 02 / Kill Abstract Slogans
  State your service plainly: clarity always converts higher than cleverness.

• 03 / Place Product Screenshot in Hero
  Show the actual dashboard or website interface—never leave it to imagination.
