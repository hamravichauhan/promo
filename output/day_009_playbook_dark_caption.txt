DAY 09 / 100: Native Lazy Loading: Saving Megabytes of Data on Mobile

Never download assets the user hasn't scrolled to yet.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / Add loading='lazy' to Below-the-Fold
  Images below the screen will wait until the user scrolls within 500px.

• 02 / Mark Hero Image 'fetchpriority=high'
  Tells the browser to download the primary hero image before anything else.

• 03 / Lazy-Load YouTube & Map Iframes
  Save 800KB of tracking scripts by lazy-loading third-party video embeds.
