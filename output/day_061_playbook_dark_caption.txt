DAY 61 / 100: The Golden Ratio of Web Typography: Hierarchy That Guides the Eye

When everything looks bold and important, nothing looks important.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / Limit Body Line Length to 65 Chars
  Overly wide text lines strain the eyes as they travel back to find the next sentence.

• 02 / High Contrast Colors
  Never use light gray text on white backgrounds. Ensure minimum WCAG AAA 7:1 contrast.

• 03 / Maximum 2 Font Families
  Use one expressive font for headlines and one ultra-legible neutral font for body copy.
