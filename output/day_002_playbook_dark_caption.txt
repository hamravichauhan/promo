DAY 02 / 100: Why You Must Stop Uploading 5MB PNGs Today

Modern web formats deliver identical visual fidelity at a fraction of the file size.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / Use Squoosh or Sharp
  Batch convert design exports to WebP at 85% quality—human eyes cannot spot the difference.

• 02 / Use Responsive Picture Tags
  Serve 600px widths to mobile and 1800px to desktop using HTML picture elements.

• 03 / Enable CDN Auto-Format
  Cloudflare and Cloudinary automatically negotiate AVIF to supporting browsers.
