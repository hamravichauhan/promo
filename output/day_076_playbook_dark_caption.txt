DAY 76 / 100: Multi-Step Forms: The Psychology of Micro-Commitments

Breaking a long form into 3 bite-sized steps increases completions by up to 86%.

Key takeaways to apply to your website today:

• 01 / Start with Frictionless Clicks
  Step 1 should only ask for multiple-choice button clicks—no typing required.

• 02 / Show a Visual Progress Bar
  A clear 'Step 1 of 3' bar triggers the psychological desire to complete what was started.

• 03 / Ask for Contact Info Last
  Users are far more likely to provide email after investing time in the first 2 steps.
